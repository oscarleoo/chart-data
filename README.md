# chart-data

The data behind the charts at [oscarleo.substack.com](https://oscarleo.substack.com).

One folder per chart. Every number is checked by hand against the primary source
before the chart is published; the CSV here is exactly the data the chart renders.

| Chart | Data | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Universities built AI. Then they were priced out. | [`ai-academia-exit.csv`](ai-academia-exit/ai-academia-exit.csv) | Epoch AI, notable AI models database |
| Chocolate is the new coffee | [`chocolate-next-coffee.csv`](chocolate-next-coffee/chocolate-next-coffee.csv) | Eurostat, HICP monthly index CP01183 (chocolate) |
| No Western country has seen coffee prices rise like Sweden | [`coffee-sweden.csv`](coffee-sweden/coffee-sweden.csv) | Eurostat, HICP monthly index (coffee), European countries |
| And 2026 is falling even faster | [`crime-2026-acceleration.csv`](crime-2026-acceleration/crime-2026-acceleration.csv) | FBI Quarterly Uniform Crime Report, H1 2026 preliminary (vs H1 2025) |
| America's egg fever broke. Europe's hasn't. | [`eggs-fever.csv`](eggs-fever/eggs-fever.csv) | Eurostat, HICP monthly index CP01147 (eggs), ~35 countries; US BLS, CPI eggs (series CUUR0000SEFH) |
| America's murder rate hit a record low in 2025 | [`murder-rate-low.csv`](murder-rate-low/murder-rate-low.csv) | FBI Uniform Crime Reporting, national estimates 1960-2019; FBI annual releases, press-verified figures 2020-2025; Statistics Canada, table 35-10-0068 (homicide rate, 1961-2025) |
| America's obesity curve just bent | [`obesity-bend.csv`](obesity-bend/obesity-bend.csv) | CDC/NCHS, NHANES (measured obesity prevalence, 1960-2023); Gallup National Health and Well-Being Index (2022-2026) |
| The olive oil fever has broken | [`olive-oil-fever.csv`](olive-oil-fever/olive-oil-fever.csv) | Eurostat, HICP monthly index CP01153 (olive oil) |
| Property crime in America is the lowest ever measured | [`property-crime-low.csv`](property-crime-low/property-crime-low.csv) | FBI Uniform Crime Reporting, national property crime rate estimates 1960-2024; FBI 2025 release (percentage change applied to the 2024 rate) |
| Grades go up, skills go down | [`top-marks-falling-skills.csv`](top-marks-falling-skills/top-marks-falling-skills.csv) | Harvard Office of Undergraduate Education / Harvard Crimson (flat-A share); Yale University grade reports; HESA (UK first-class honours), JCQ (A-level A*/A share); ACT research reports (self-reported HSGPA); Rojstaczer & Healy, gradeinflation.com; OECD Survey of Adult Skills (PIAAC), 2012 and 2023 cycles |
| America's violent crime rate just fell at record pace | [`violent-crime-low.csv`](violent-crime-low/violent-crime-low.csv) | FBI Uniform Crime Reporting, national violent crime rate estimates 1960-2025 |

Licensing: the underlying data belongs to the cited sources; the compilation is
